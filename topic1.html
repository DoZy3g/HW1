Governing equation

I(x, α₀) = I₀ · [ 1 − (1 − e−k·x) · e−α₀² / (2α_c²) ]

x = mass-thickness (ρt, relative units; 0.5 = thin, 1.8 = thick)
α₀ = objective aperture half-angle (mrad)
α_c = characteristic scattering angle (mrad; smaller at higher kV)
k = effective scattering coefficient (set to 0.7 for the soft-material model)

At small α₀ most scattered electrons are blocked → thick/dense regions appear dark → high contrast. As α₀ grows, scattered electrons re-enter the BF beam → intensity difference shrinks → contrast falls. Higher kV reduces α_c → scattering is more forward-peaked → a given aperture collects more scattered electrons → contrast decreases.
